Questions

I used it during a cooldown.

That's fine. Your history marks it as used during a cooldown, and the cooldown starts over from that moment. It counts toward your compliance rate and break count in Stats.

How are savings calculated?

If you enter an estimated cost for an item, the app estimates how many uses the cooldown saved you this month and shows that amount. It's an estimate, not an exact figure, and items without an estimated cost are left out.

I'm not getting notifications.

On your iPhone, check Settings > Notifications > CoolTime. If the app's first screen says notifications are off, the Settings button there takes you straight to it.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap + at the top left, find CoolTime and add it. There are Lock Screen widgets too. They show what you can use now and the time left.

How do I delete my records?

Deleting an item also deletes its records. Deleting the app removes all of them. Records live only on your device, so once deleted they can't be recovered.

Does it sync to my other devices?

Not yet. Your records live on this one iPhone. Sync across devices and backups are on the list in Settings > Coming Features.

Is it really free?

Yes. Nothing is locked behind a payment. If you like the app you can send the developer a tip from Settings, but nothing changes if you don't.

Are my records sent anywhere?

No. There's no account and no ads, and your cooldowns and history are stored only on your iPhone. The developer receives only anonymous usage statistics, error reports and any feedback you send yourself. The Privacy Policy below has the details.

I found a bug or want a feature.

Tell us in the app under Settings > Support > Send Feedback, by Instagram DM at @lee25_ios, or by email below. If a screen got stuck, mention what you were doing and your iOS version and we'll find it much faster.
