Cooldowns for your impulses

Like a skill cooldown in a game, you set a waiting time on spending and habits. Food delivery once every 3 days, clothes shopping once every 2 weeks. After you use something, a ring slowly fills until you can use it again.

🍔

How it works

Create a cooldown

Pick a name, an emoji and how long to wait (hours, days, weeks or months). For common items like food, shopping, entertainment, health and finance, add one from the recommended templates with a single tap.

Use it

Tap Use it when you use something and its cooldown starts over. You can add a note about why and the actual cost. If you use it during a cooldown, that gets recorded too.

Get notified when it ends

You get a notification when a cooldown ends. The home screen widget shows what you can use right now and how much time is left.

Stats

See how well you kept your cooldowns (compliance rate), records by category, how many times you broke a cooldown, and this month's savings for items with an estimated cost.

Your records stay on your device

No accounts, no ads. Your cooldowns and usage history are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer. The only things that reach the developer are anonymous usage statistics, error reports and feedback you send yourself. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
