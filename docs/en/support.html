FAQ

I used it during a cooldown

That's fine. The usage log marks it as used during a cooldown, and the cooldown starts over from that moment. It counts toward your compliance rate and break count in Stats.

How are savings calculated?

If you enter an estimated cost for an item, the app estimates how many uses the cooldown saved you this month and shows the amount. It's an estimate, not an exact figure, and items without an estimated cost are left out.

I'm not getting notifications

On your iPhone, check that notifications are on under Settings → Notifications → CoolTime. If the app's first screen says notifications are off, the Settings button there takes you straight to it.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap + at the top left, find CoolTime and add it. It shows what you can use now and the time left.

How do I delete my records?

Deleting an item also deletes its records. Deleting the app removes all records. Records exist only on your device, so deleted records can't be recovered.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, and your records are stored only on your device. The developer receives only anonymous usage statistics and error reports. See the Privacy Policy for details.
